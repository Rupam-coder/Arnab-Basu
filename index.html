01 // Convergence

Where spirituality meets the workplace, studied with rigor.

I am an academician, researcher, and writer investigating the human interior within organizational structures. Currently serving as Academic Coordinator at Techno India University (involved with the School of the Future) and pursuing my PhD in Management at UEM Kolkata.

My scholarship focuses on modern spiritual transitions—particularly the "Spiritual But Not Religious" (SBNR) movement—and its measurable impact on employee mental health, resilience, and workplace spirituality. Using rigorous methodologies such as PRISMA systematic reviews, my work evaluates spiritual interventions for older adults suffering from dementia, as well as the physiological health and performance outcomes of working women.

Prior to higher education, I spent years in corporate systems and market operations at Tata Consultancy Services, Torero Corporation, and Asian Paints. That foundation anchors my empirical social science in lived organizational reality.

Location Kolkata, West Bengal, India
Current Appointments Academic Coordinator, Techno India University PhD Scholar (Management), UEM Kolkata
Methodologies PRISMA Systematic Reviews, Empirical Surveys, Quantitative Synthesis
